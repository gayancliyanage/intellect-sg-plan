LinkedIn Strategy

LinkedIn Sales Navigator Playbook

LinkedIn is the primary hunting ground for Singapore CIOs and IT decision-makers. Run this systematically.

🔧 Setup (Week 5, Day 1)

  • 1. Subscribe to LinkedIn Sales Navigator (Team plan recommended)
  • 2. Set company page to: Intellect Consulting — update logo, about, services
  • 3. Personal profile: Add "SAP Consulting | Singapore" to headline
  • 4. Create a Lead List named "SG CIO Targets Q1 2025"

🔍 Search Filter Configuration

  • → Geography: Singapore
  • → Job Titles: CIO, VP IT, Head of IT, Head of Digital Transformation, Head of Enterprise Applications, SAP Programme Manager, IT Director, VP Technology, Chief Digital Officer
  • → Company Size: 500+ employees
  • → Industries: Manufacturing, Logistics, Food & Beverages, Financial Services, Oil & Gas, Chemicals
  • → Keywords: SAP, S/4HANA, digital transformation, ERP

LinkedIn Outreach Sequence

A 3-touch sequence over 14 days. Don't pitch immediately — build context first.

Day 1 — Connection Request

Hi [Name] — I noticed your work at [Company] on [specific initiative if visible]. I lead SAP consulting at Intellect Consulting, helping companies in Singapore with S/4HANA and digital transformation. Would love to connect. — [Your Name]
Day 7 — First Message (After Connecting)

Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!

I'm reaching out because Intellect Consulting specialises in SAP S/4HANA — particularly for [their industry]. Given the 2027 ECC end-of-support deadline, I imagine [Company] is evaluating the migration path.

We've helped similar companies navigate this. Happy to share what we've seen work well in this region — no pitch, just a conversation.

Would a 20-min call make sense? — [Your Name]
Day 14 — Follow-Up (No Response)

Hi [Name], following up briefly.

Totally understand if the timing isn't right. If there's ever a need for SAP specialist support — implementation, module expertise, or just a second opinion on your roadmap — we'd be glad to help.

Saving this for when it makes sense. All the best with [Company]'s plans! — [Your Name]

Singapore's leading tech trade association with 1,400+ member companies ranging from MNCs to startups. It is the single best networking organisation for B2B tech in Singapore.

  • → CIO Chapter events (closed-door, senior-only)
  • → Digital Transformation roundtables

🤝

How to Network (Without Pitching)

Singapore's business culture values relationships before business. At events:

  • → Ask "what's your biggest IT challenge right now?" — listen, don't pitch
  • → Share insights about the SAP 2027 deadline — be the expert
  • → Follow up on LinkedIn within 24 hours
  • → Second meeting: offer a free 30-min SAP readiness assessment
  • → Third meeting: present Intellect's services
