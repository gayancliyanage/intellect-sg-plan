Why Singapore

The Right Market at the Right Time

🏦 Regional HQ Hub

Over 37,000 international companies have offices in Singapore. Many run SAP as their regional ERP backbone — creating constant demand for specialist consultants and implementation support.

💰 Government Digital Grants

IMDA's SMEs Go Digital programme offers up to 50% subsidies for digital solutions. GenAI Navigator provides grants for AI adoption. Being a pre-approved vendor means warm, funded inbound leads.

🤖 AI Investment Surge

Singapore's National AI Strategy 2.0 targets SGD 1 billion in AI investment. Every major enterprise is evaluating AI — creating fresh demand for technology and AI consultation services.

🌏 ASEAN Gateway

A Singapore presence is the standard credential for doing business across ASEAN. Clients in Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam all look to Singapore-based partners for regional rollouts.

🏛️ EDB & Government Support

Singapore's Economic Development Board actively assists foreign companies establishing here. EDB can facilitate introductions to target companies and government agencies.

⚡ SAP Migration Wave

SAP ECC (old ERP) reaches end of support in 2027. Every Singapore company running ECC must migrate to S/4HANA — creating a multi-year wave of implementation projects that needs specialist talent.
