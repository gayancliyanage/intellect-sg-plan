IMDA Registration

SMEs Go Digital — Pre-Approved Vendor

IMDA (Infocomm Media Development Authority) runs Singapore's national SME digitalisation programme. Being a pre-approved vendor means warm, grant-funded inbound leads.

💡

What is SMEs Go Digital?

A government programme helping Singapore SMEs adopt digital solutions. IMDA pre-approves a list of vendors and digital solutions. SMEs using pre-approved vendors receive up to 50% government grant subsidy on their digital project costs.

This means Intellect's clients pay half price — dramatically lowering the barrier to engagement.

🤖

GenAI Navigator Programme

IMDA's AI-specific programme that recommends pre-approved AI solutions to SMEs with up to 50% grant support. If Intellect develops an AI consultation or GenAI implementation offering, registration here creates warm inbound AI leads.

IMDA Registration — Step by Step

1

Register on CTO-as-a-Service (CTOaaS)

Visit services2.imda.gov.sg/CTOaaS. Create a vendor account for Intellect Consulting. This is the self-help platform where SMEs discover and engage vendors.

2

Identify Your Solutions Category

Select the appropriate category for Intellect's services: ERP (SAP), IT Consulting, AI Solutions, or Digital Transformation. Each category has specific approval criteria.

3

Submit Pre-Qualification Application

Provide company registration (ACRA), financial statements, 3 client references with testimonials, details of your solution/service offering, and pricing structure.

4

IMDA Assessment

IMDA reviews your application (typically 4–8 weeks). They may request a demo or additional references. Prepare a strong submission upfront to avoid back-and-forth.

5

Listed on Pre-Approved Vendor Directory

Once approved, Intellect appears in the IMDA vendor directory. SMEs browsing for SAP/AI/tech vendors find you with grant subsidy attached. Warm, funded inbound leads.

⚠️ Important: Singapore Entity Required

IMDA pre-approval requires a Singapore-registered company (ACRA registration). If Intellect doesn't have a Singapore entity yet, this needs to happen first. ACRA registration takes 1–3 business days online via BizFile+. A Singapore-registered entity also opens doors to EDB support, SGTech membership, and government tenders.

Government Procurement

GeBIZ — Government Electronic Business

🏛️

What is GeBIZ?

Singapore's government e-procurement portal. All government ministries, agencies, and statutory boards (GovTech, HDB, JTC, LTA, PUB, etc.) post their IT procurement on GeBIZ. Being registered is mandatory for government work — and government contracts provide stable, recurring revenue.

Register on GeBIZ →
📋

Registration Steps

  • 1. Register on GeBIZ Supplier Portal using Singapore entity (UEN number)
  • 2. Select relevant UNSPSC commodity codes for IT Consulting, ERP, Professional Services
  • 3. Upload ACRA registration, bank details, company profile
  • 4. Set up bid alert notifications for SAP, ERP, IT Consulting tenders
  • 5. Monitor GeBIZ weekly for relevant ITQs (Invitation to Quote) and ITTs (Invitation to Tender)

EDB Singapore

Economic Development Board

🌐

Why EDB?

Singapore's EDB is one of the world's most effective investment promotion agencies. They actively help foreign companies establish in Singapore — connecting you with potential clients, government agencies, and industry networks.

Position Intellect as a foreign tech company establishing a Southeast Asia base in Singapore. EDB can open doors that would otherwise take years.

Contact EDB →
🤝

What EDB Offers

  • → Introductions to companies in target sectors
  • → Market entry advisory and connections
  • → Potential grants for establishing Singapore operations
  • → Access to government agency introductions
  • → Ecosystem connections (industry associations, chambers)
  • → Enterprise Development Grant (EDG) eligibility
Speaking Opportunities

Build Public Authority

Why Speaking Matters in Singapore

Singapore's business culture highly values expertise signals. A speaking slot at an SGTech event or SAP conference positions Intellect's leadership as thought leaders — infinitely more powerful than advertising. Aim for Month 3 pitch, delivery in Month 4–5.

SGTech Chapter Events

Pitch a 20-min talk on "SAP S/4HANA migration — what Singapore CIOs need to know before 2027". Contact SGTech's events team after joining as a member.

SAP Community Singapore

SAP runs user group events in Singapore. Pitch a case study presentation — showing how a food manufacturer or logistics company benefited from your SAP work.

IMDA Workshops

IMDA regularly runs SME digitalisation workshops. As a pre-approved vendor, you can pitch to run or co-run a session on ERP modernisation or AI for SMEs.
